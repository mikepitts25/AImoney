# 1. COLD CALL SCRIPT
# =============================================================

## BEFORE YOU DIAL -- Quick Prep (30 seconds)

- Pull up the company's Google Business Profile
- Note their star rating and number of reviews
- Note their hours (are they closed on weekends? evenings?)
- Check if they have a "Book Online" option or just a phone number
- Write down the owner's first name

---

## SCENARIO A: THEY ANSWER

### Opening Hook (First 10 Seconds)

> **You:** Hey [First Name], this is [Mike/Paola] with Colibri Code. I'll be real quick -- I work with [HVAC/plumbing/roofing] companies in [their city/state], and I had a quick question. Do you have 60 seconds?

**Why this works:**
- Uses their first name (pattern interrupt -- "who is this?")
- "I'll be real quick" reduces resistance (40% fewer hang-ups when you set a time expectation)
- "I had a quick question" triggers curiosity -- people answer questions reflexively
- 60 seconds is a small ask

### If They Say "Yeah, go ahead" or "What's up?"

### Problem Agitation (20 seconds)

> **You:** So I've been talking to a lot of [HVAC/plumbing] owners around [city], and the number one thing I keep hearing is -- they're losing jobs because calls come in while they're on a ladder, under a house, or it's after hours, and by the time they call back, that customer already booked someone else. Is that something you run into at all?

**Why this works:**
- "Talking to a lot of owners" -- social proof, you're not just cold calling random people
- "On a ladder, under a house" -- vivid, relatable language specific to their trade
- Ends with an open question -- gets them talking

### If They Confirm (Most Will)

### Solution Teaser (20 seconds)

> **You:** Yeah, that's exactly what we help with. So what we built is an AI phone agent -- basically it answers your calls 24/7, sounds like a real person, books the appointment right onto your calendar, and sends you a text with the details. Your phone never stops ringing, but you never miss a job either.

> The reason I'm calling is we just helped a [plumbing/HVAC] company in [nearby city] pick up an extra [$X,000] a month just from the calls they were missing. I'd love to show you a quick demo of how it sounds -- it takes about 15 minutes. Would [Tuesday or Thursday] work better for you?

**Why this works:**
- "Sounds like a real person" -- addresses the #1 fear before they ask
- "Books the appointment right onto your calendar" -- concrete benefit
- "Sends you a text with the details" -- they stay in control
- Case study with dollar amount anchors the value
- Two-option close ("Tuesday or Thursday") is easier to answer than "when are you free?"

### If They Say Yes

> **You:** Awesome. Let me grab your email so I can send over a calendar invite -- what's the best one? Perfect. You'll get that in the next few minutes. And [First Name], I'm going to have the AI call you before our meeting so you can hear it yourself. Talk soon.

## OBJECTION HANDLING

### "I'm not interested."

> **You:** Totally fair -- and I wouldn't expect you to be interested in something you haven't seen yet. Most of the guys I work with said the same thing at first. Can I ask you one thing though? How many calls a week would you guess you're missing while you're on a job?

> *(If they engage, loop back to pain. If they're firm, say:)* No worries at all, [Name]. If you ever want to check it out, I'm easy to find. Have a good one.

### "I already have a receptionist."

> **You:** That's great -- you're ahead of most companies out there. Out of curiosity, does she work nights and weekends too? ... Yeah, so what a lot of our clients do is keep their receptionist for daytime and use our AI for after-hours and overflow -- the calls that come in when she's on another line, at lunch, or the business is closed. It actually takes pressure off your team. Would it be worth seeing how that works?

### "How much does it cost?"

> **You:** Great question. It depends on how you want it set up, but most of our clients are in the $300 to $500 a month range -- which, if you think about it, is about what one missed HVAC job costs you. We usually pay for ourselves in the first week. But honestly, the best thing is to see it first so you know what you're getting. Can we do a quick 15-minute demo?

**Key:** Never give exact pricing on a cold call. Give a range, immediately anchor to ROI, redirect to the demo.

### "I don't trust AI."

> **You:** I hear you, and honestly, a year ago I would have said the same thing. Here's what changed my mind -- I'll have it call you right now, and you tell me if you can tell it's AI. If it doesn't sound right, you'll never hear from me again. Fair enough?

> *(If they agree, trigger a demo call. This is your strongest close for skeptics.)*

### "Just send me an email."

> **You:** Absolutely, I'll do that. What's the best email? ... Got it. And [Name], I'll include a link so you can hear a sample call -- it's 90 seconds. If it sounds like something that could help, just hit reply and we'll set something up. Sound good?

**Key:** Always get the email. Always include a sample call recording or link in the email. This objection is often a polite brush-off, but the sample call can re-engage them.

## PRE-CALL PREP (2 minutes before the call)

- Review their website and Google reviews
- Note their service area, services offered, team size
- Have their phone number ready to trigger a live demo call
- Have the demo environment loaded and ready to screen share
- Have the ROI calculator ready with their trade pre-selected

## MINUTE 0-2: Rapport + Agenda Setting

> **You:** Hey [Name], thanks for taking the time. Before we jump in, I just want to set a quick agenda so I don't waste your time. I want to ask a couple questions about how your phones work right now, then show you a live demo of the AI agent -- you'll actually hear it take a call -- and then we can talk about whether it makes sense for your business. Sound good?

> *(Small talk -- reference something specific: "I saw you guys have a 4.8 on Google, that's impressive" or "Looks like you've been in business for 15 years, that's legit.")*

**Why the agenda matters:** Contractors respect people who don't waste their time. Setting the agenda positions you as a professional, not a pitch person.

---

## MINUTE 2-5: Discovery Questions (Uncover Pain)

Ask these in a natural conversation, not like a checklist:

### Must-Ask Questions:

1. **"How many calls would you say you get in a typical week?"**
   *(Establishes baseline volume. Most HVAC/plumbing companies get 30-100 calls/week.)*

2. **"And of those, how many do you think you're actually able to answer live?"**
   *(This is where the pain lives. Most will admit 60-70%. Some will say less.)*

3. **"What happens when you miss one -- does someone call them back?"**
   *(Reveals their current process. Often it's "I try to call back when I get a chance.")*

4. **"How do you handle after-hours and weekend calls right now?"**
   *(Most answer "voicemail." This is your opening.)*

5. **"When you do get a new customer on the phone, what's the average job worth to you?"**
   *(Gets THEM to state the value. This number anchors the entire ROI conversation.)*

### Bonus Questions (If Time Allows):

6. "Do you use any scheduling software right now?" *(Identifies integration opportunities)*
7. "Who on your team is answering phones -- is it you, office staff, or a service?" *(Identifies if there's a gatekeeper or decision-maker dynamic)*
8. "Are you busier in certain seasons?" *(Seasonal businesses have worse missed-call problems during peaks)*

### Transition to Demo:

> **You:** So if I'm hearing you right, you're getting about [X] calls a week, missing maybe [Y] of those, and each job is worth around [$Z] to you. That means you could be leaving [$Y x $Z] on the table every single week. Let me show you how we fix that -- I'm going to have the AI call your phone right now.

---

## MINUTE 5-10: Live Demo

### The Demo Structure:

**Step 1: Trigger the call**

> **You:** Okay, I'm going to have it call you right now. Just answer like you're a customer calling for service. Say whatever you'd normally hear -- "My AC went out," "I have a leak under my sink," whatever. Ready?

*(Trigger the demo call to their cell phone. Let them experience it as a caller.)*

**Step 2: While they're on the AI call (you're on mute), prepare the dashboard view.**

**Step 3: After the call ends (60-90 seconds):**

> **You:** So what did you think? ... Yeah, most people are surprised by how natural it sounds. Now let me show you what happened on the backend.

*(Share screen and show:)*
- The call summary that was generated
- The appointment that was booked on the calendar
- The text notification that was sent to their phone
- The lead details captured (name, address, service needed, urgency)

**Step 4: Reinforce the value:**

> **You:** So imagine that happening at 9 PM on a Saturday when someone's AC dies. Instead of getting your voicemail and calling your competitor, they just booked with you. And you wake up Sunday morning with the appointment already on your calendar.

---

## MINUTE 10-13: Pricing Presentation

### Transition:

> **You:** So let me walk you through what this looks like investment-wise, and I'll be upfront -- I'm going to show you the math on why this basically pays for itself.

### Present the ROI First, Price Second:

> **You:** You told me each job is worth about [$Z] and you're missing roughly [Y] calls a week. Even if the AI converts just 3 or 4 of those into booked jobs per month, that's [$Z x 3-4] in revenue you're currently losing. Our service runs [$X/month] -- so you're looking at a [10-15x] return on that investment.

### Then Present the Price:

> **You:** We have a couple of options. Most [HVAC/plumbing] companies our size go with the [recommended tier], which runs [$X/month]. That includes [list 3-4 key features]. There's also a [lower tier] at [$Y/month] if you want to start smaller, and a [premium tier] at [$Z/month] that includes [premium features].

*(See Section 3 for exact pricing tiers and what to include.)*

### Handle "Let me think about it":

> **You:** Totally understand. Can I ask -- what's the main thing you'd want to think through? Is it the price, or is it more about whether the technology works for your business?

*(This identifies the real objection so you can address it.)*

---

## MINUTE 13-15: Close or Next Steps

### If They're Ready:

> **You:** Great, so here's how we get started. I'll send over a simple agreement, and then [Mike/Paola] and I will set up a 30-minute onboarding call where we build your AI agent together -- we'll program it with your services, pricing, service area, and calendar. Most clients are live within 48 hours. Want to lock in a setup time this week?

### If They Need to "Talk to a Partner" or "Think About It":

> **You:** That makes total sense. Here's what I'd suggest -- I'll send you a summary with the pricing and a recording of the demo call so you can share it with [partner's name]. Can we set a follow-up call for [specific day] so I can answer any questions? Even if the answer is no, that way it's not hanging over your head.

**Key:** ALWAYS set a specific follow-up date. Never leave it at "I'll get back to you."

### Closing Line:

> **You:** [Name], I appreciate your time. Honestly, the way I see it -- every day without this, there are calls going to voicemail that could be jobs on your calendar. The sooner we get you set up, the sooner those calls stop slipping through. I'll send everything over today.

### WHEN TO OFFER DISCOUNTS vs. HOLD FIRM

**HOLD FIRM when:**
- They see the value but are just negotiating out of habit
- They're comparing you to cheap AI tools (different product)
- They have the budget -- they're just testing you
- It's a large company that can clearly afford it

**Offer a modest discount when:**
- They're a great reference account (first client in a new trade or city)
- They commit to an annual plan
- They refer another business that signs up
- They're genuinely a small 1-2 person shop and the Starter tier is a stretch

**Never discount below:**
- Starter: $247/month
- Growth: $397/month
- Premium: $647/month

**Instead of discounting, add value:**
- "I can't go lower on the monthly, but I'll waive the setup fee."
- "What if I include the Spanish-language option at no extra charge for the first 3 months?"
- "I'll throw in a custom integration with your scheduling software."

# 5. ROI CALCULATOR TALKING POINTS

## THE FORMULA

```
Monthly Revenue Lost = Missed Calls/Month x Close Rate x Average Job Value

Monthly ROI = Revenue Recovered - Monthly Service Cost
```

## INDUSTRY BENCHMARKS TO USE IN CALCULATIONS

| Metric | HVAC | Plumbing | Roofing |
|--------|------|----------|---------|
| Avg calls/week | 50-80 | 40-70 | 30-50 |
| Missed call rate | 28-40% | 28-40% | 30-45% |
| Avg missed calls/month | 56-128 | 45-112 | 36-90 |
| Avg service/repair job value | $400-$1,200 | $300-$500 | $8,000-$15,000 |
| Avg job value (blended w/ installs) | $1,400-$1,800 | $445-$800 | $11,000-$15,000 |
| Close rate on inbound calls | 50-60% | 50-60% | 30-40% |
| % of missed callers who call competitor | 62% | 62% | 62% |
| % who never call back after voicemail | 80-85% | 80-85% | 80-85% |

---
